# Campaña del Rosario

Digital inventory for the Campaña del Rosario: tracks where every pilgrim image is, who holds it, and who it has been held by — replacing the spreadsheets the campaign ran on before.

## Language

### Territory

Three nested levels, from widest to narrowest. A Peregrina and a Misionero each sit at all three.

**Región**:
One of the campaign's seven pastoral regions covering the country: `NOA`, `CENTRO`, `CUYO`, `NEA`, `BS. AS`, `R. PAM`, `R. PAT`. The widest territorial level.
_Avoid_: Zona, Área

**Provincia**:
An Argentine province, within a Región. Contributes its abbreviation to a Peregrina's Código.

**Diócesis/Localidad**:
The narrowest territorial level — the diocese or town a Peregrina and Misionero belong to.
_Avoid_: Parroquia (that is a Centro, not a territory)

### People

**Usuario**:
A person who signs in. Every Usuario holds exactly one Rol. Distinct from a Misionero, who never signs in.
_Avoid_: Cuenta, Miembro

**Actor**:
The authenticated Usuario on whose behalf an operation runs — their Rol plus the territory that bounds what they may see and change.
_Avoid_: Session, Current user

**Rol**:
One of four, ranked: `admin`, `asesor_nacional`, `responsable_diocesano`, `referente_local`. A Usuario may only create and manage Usuarios of a strictly lower Rol.

**Asesor Nacional**:
A Usuario with visibility over the whole country, who manages Responsables Diocesanos.
_Avoid_: Admin (that is a separate, higher Rol)

**Responsable Diocesano**:
A Usuario scoped to one Diócesis/Localidad, who manages the Referentes Locales within it.
_Avoid_: Zone manager, Encargado diocesano

**Referente Local**:
A Usuario who registers Misioneros and assigns Peregrinas to them within their own territory.
_Avoid_: Encargado de Rama, Local manager

**Misionero**:
A person who takes charge of a Peregrina. A tracked data entity only — a Misionero has no credentials and never signs in.
_Avoid_: Usuario, Voluntario

### Objects

**Peregrina**:
A pilgrim image tracked by the campaign.

**Código**:
The identifier written on a Peregrina, generated by the system as `[Provincia Modalidad Número]` — e.g. `CBA JOV 0001`. The número runs sequentially within each Provincia and Modalidad pair. Never typed by hand, and never parsed to decide permissions.
_Avoid_: ID String

**Modalidad**:
The branch of the campaign a Peregrina or Misionero belongs to: `JOV` (Jóvenes), `FAM` (Familias), `INF` (Infancia), `ADU` (Adultos).
_Avoid_: Rama, Categoría

**Tipo**:
Whether a Peregrina is a `peregrina` or an `auxiliar`. Independent of Modalidad.
_Avoid_: Modalidad

**Estado**:
The condition of a Peregrina: Activa, En reparación, or Extraviada. It describes the image and says nothing about who has it — an Extraviada Peregrina keeps its open Asignación, because the last holder is where anyone starts looking. `Inactiva` is a legacy value: still displayed on records that carry it, never offered for new entry.
_Avoid_: using it to mean "not currently assigned" (that is Tenencia actual), or "out of service" (that is De baja)

**Centro**:
The place where a Peregrina is venerated — a santuario, ermita, or parroquia, recorded with its name.
_Avoid_: Ubicación, Sede

**Asignación**:
A period during which one Misionero had charge of one Peregrina, recorded with who registered it. Closed when the Peregrina is returned or handed on.
_Avoid_: Ubicación, Préstamo

**Tenencia actual**:
The single open Asignación for a Peregrina — the Misionero responsible for it right now. A Peregrina has at most one, and no Asignación is ever overwritten: handing an image on closes one period and opens another.
_Avoid_: Ubicación

**De baja**:
Retired rather than deleted. A Peregrina permanently out of service, a Misionero who has left the Campaña, or a Usuario whose access has ended — recorded with a timestamp, excluded from active lists, and never destroyed, because every Asignación has to keep resolving to a real Código and a real name. Refused while an Asignación is still open: an image physically in somebody's house has not left the inventory.
_Avoid_: Eliminar, Borrar, Baja (on its own, for a Peregrina that is merely unassigned)

**Año de consagración**:
The year a Misionero was consecrated.

**Resumen anual**:
A free-text account, kept per calendar year, of a Misionero's activity with their Peregrina.
